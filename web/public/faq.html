Frequently asked questions

Everything about Card-O-Rama, the AI trading card scanner & appraiser.

What is Card-O-Rama?

Point your phone or webcam at a trading card and it identifies the card, estimates its value, reads its condition, and suggests smart trades — then helps you track your collection over time.

Which cards does it work with?

Pokémon, baseball, basketball, football, soccer, cricket, and hockey — modern and vintage, including parallels, autos, relics, rookies, short prints, and serial-numbered cards.

Is it free?

Yes — you can scan, appraise, build a binder and wishlist, check trades, and get the morning briefing for free.

How do I scan a card?

Open Scan, use your camera or upload a photo, and line the card up inside the on-screen frame — it doesn't have to fill the whole screen, we crop to the box. For the sharpest read, use good lighting and hold steady; the app flags blurry shots so you can retake.

I don't have the card in hand — can I still look it up?

Yes. Use Search and just describe the card (player, set, year, parallel) to get the same value, stats, and trade ideas.

How accurate are the values?

Values and outlooks are AI estimates based on your photo plus live data — a smart starting point, not a live market feed. When the app isn't sure it says so, and it flags anything that looks like a reprint or fake. Always sanity-check before a big trade.

What is the morning briefing?

A daily update on your players and cards — real, dated results and trends across the sports and TCGs you follow. Sections about your binder and wishlist only appear once you actually have cards in them.

How is my data stored?

Your account and collection live in your browser on your device, not on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I forgot my password.

On the login screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email — we'll send a reset code to the address on your account. (Password reset works on the device where your account lives.)

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — it's a web app, so it runs in your phone's browser. You can also add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.
