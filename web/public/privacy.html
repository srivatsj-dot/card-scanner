Privacy Policy

Card-O-Rama. Last updated: 2026.

Card-O-Rama (“we”, “the app”) is a trading-card scanner and appraiser. This page explains what data we handle and why. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What stays on your device

Your account (username, a securely hashed password, and email), your binder, wishlist, trades, settings, and saved card photos are stored locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

What we send for processing

  • Card analysis: when you scan or search a card, the photo or text you provide is sent to Google’s Gemini AI to identify and appraise the card. It is processed to generate your result and not used to build a profile of you.
  • Live data: to value cards and build the daily briefing we query public services (e.g. eBay, official sports data feeds, ESPN, Limitless TCG). Only the card/query terms are sent.
  • Email: if email is enabled, your address is used only to send you a welcome message and password-reset codes, via an email provider (e.g. Gmail/your configured service).

Cookies & advertising

If advertising is enabled, we use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads based on your prior activity. You can control ad personalization at Google Ad Settings and learn more at Google’s advertising policies. In the EEA/UK we request consent before serving personalized ads.

Analytics

If enabled, we use Google Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymous usage (e.g. how many people sign up) so we can improve the app.

Your choices

You can delete your account and all its local data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account. Clearing your browser data also removes everything stored locally.

Children

Card-O-Rama is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
