Privacy Policy

Card-O-Rama · last updated 2026.

Card-O-Rama is made for kids who love trading cards. Collecting should be fun and safe, so here is exactly what happens with your information — in plain language, kept to the bare minimum on purpose. Parents and guardians are welcome to read this together with their kids.

The short version

Card-O-Rama collects as little as possible. Your cards, lists, and settings live on your own device (or, if you turn on an account, in your private account so you can see them on your other devices). Your information is never sold, and it is only used to make the app work for you.

What stays on your device or in your account

Your binder, wishlist, trades, settings, and saved card photos are stored on your device. If you create an account to sync across devices, that same information — plus your username, email, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password — is kept in your private account so you can sign in elsewhere. Passwords are stored scrambled and can't be read back.

What gets sent out to make a card work

  • Reading a card: when you scan or describe a card, that photo or text is sent to Google's Gemini AI to identify and price it. It is used to give you a result — not to build a profile of you.
  • Looking up prices and news: card values and the morning briefing come from public sources (like eBay, official sports data, and Pokémon card databases). Only the card or search words are sent.
  • Email: if you give an email, it is used only to send a welcome message and password-reset codes — nothing else.

Made for kids — extra care

Because Card-O-Rama is for children, the goal is to gather as little personal information as possible and never anything beyond what the app needs to work. Card-O-Rama does not ask kids for their real name, address, phone number, or location, and it does not let kids post messages to other people. A parent or guardian can ask to review or delete their child's information at any time by using the contact below.

Ads

If ads are shown, they are limited to non-personalized, family-friendly ads — meaning ads are not based on tracking a child's activity across the internet. Ads are served through Google in a way intended for audiences that include children.

What is never done

Your information is never sold or rented. It is never used to advertise to you based on your private activity, and it is never shared except with the service providers needed to run the app (the AI that reads cards, the price and news sources, and the email/database services).

Deleting everything

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account. Clearing your browser also removes anything stored on the device. A parent or guardian can request deletion using the contact below.

Questions or requests

Parents, guardians, or kids can reach out any time at srivats.j@gmail.com — including to see or delete a child's information.
